In case you keep getting booted offline despite using this script, you have the option to completely block 
direct communication between players and route everything through a relay. 

To do this, you need to add the following rules to the Windows Firewall:

Inbound connections:
- Protocols and ports = UDP and all local and remote ports
- programs and services: win_path_to/GTA5_Enchanced.exe or GTA5_legacy.exe
- action: BLOCK

Outbound connections:
- Protocols and ports = UDP and all local and remote ports
- programs and services: win_path_to/GTA5_Enchanced.exe or GTA5_legacy.exe
- ip range: 
-- remote_ip 0.0.0.0-185.56.65.166
-- remote_ip 185.56.65.173-255.255.255.254
- action: BLOCK

Once this rule is set, all GTA Online traffic is forced through the relay.
Unfortunately, this can result in some latency or longer wait times.
You are no longer visible to other participants in the Social Club, 
so friends must be invited manually.

If you want to use the firewall in combination with the script, I recommend the "vanilla" version.
It focuses on outbound connections and blocks one of the known crash-inducing packets.
If you continue to experience connection drops, it is advisable to look for a session with fewer players.

Of course, this method does not provide 100% security, as it remains possible for an attacker to transmit spoofed relay packets, 
nevertheless, it significantly hinders common and well-known attack methods and increases the effort and cost for the attacker.
